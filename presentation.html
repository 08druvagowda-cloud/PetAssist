Context & Motivation
The Veterinary Healthcare & Agriculture Deficit
⚠ Critical Risk
1. Delayed Intervention
Over 70% of companion animal and livestock clinical emergencies stem from unobserved early behavioral changes. Owners lack continuous vitals tracking and miss early diagnostic warning signs.
📅 Adherence Gap
2. Broken Vaccine Adherence
Standard vaccination cards rely on physical paper records easily misplaced. Species-specific booster intervals (Rabies 365d, Bordetella 180d, FMD 180d) are routinely skipped, causing fatal preventable outbreaks.
⚡ Latency Barrier
3. Rural & Cloud Latency
Smallholder farmers and rural caretakers suffer from poor cellular connectivity and high tele-vet consult fees. When animals ingest toxic substances, waiting minutes for remote API calls leads to acute mortality.

Clinical Algorithmic Engine
Mathematical Formulation: 3-Factor Health Scoring

Mathematical Model

Score S = F_food + A_activity + B_behavior
where F, A, B ∈ {1, 2, 3} ⟹ S ∈ [3, 9]
• Food & Appetite (F): Normal (+3), Reduced (+2), Refusing (+1)
• Physical Activity (A): Active (+3), Low (+2), Lethargic (+1)
• Observed Mood (B): Playful (+3), Quiet (+2), Distressed (+1)

Clinical Diagnostic Thresholds

7 ≤ S ≤ 9 | Healthy Baseline: Optimal physiological stability. Maintain nutrition and scheduled boosters.
4 ≤ S ≤ 6 | Warning (Dip): Sub-clinical indication. Monitor hydration and log 12h vitals.
S ≤ 3 | Critical Alert: Immediate clinical escalation; one-click dispatch to Care Center.
Automated Preventative Care
Predictive Immunization Interval Calculus

Interval Calculus

D_next = D_administered + Δt_interval
Remaining Days R = D_next - D_current
• Rabies (Canine & Feline): 365 Days (Annual)
• Bordetella (Kennel Cough): 180 Days (Biannual)
• Foot & Mouth Disease - FMD (Cattle): 180 Days
• PPR (Sheep & Goats): 90 Days

Tri-State Alert Machine

State 1: Up to Date (R > 14d): Immunological protection active. Green status badge.
State 2: Due Soon (0 ≤ R ≤ 14d): Approaching booster window. Amber warning badge & booking prompt.
State 3: Overdue (R < 0d): Immunity lapsed. High contagion risk. Emergency escalation.

Enterprise Hardening
OWASP Top-10 Security Architecture
A03:2021 Mitigation
1. SQL Injection (SQLi) Defense
Zero string concatenation. 100% of database queries execute through parameterized prepared statements (`$stmt->prepare()` with explicit type binding).
A07:2021 Mitigation
2. Cross-Site Scripting (XSS)
All dynamic user inputs undergo entity encoding (`htmlspecialchars(..., ENT_QUOTES, 'UTF-8')`) before DOM rendering, neutralizing script injections.
A02:2021 Mitigation
3. Cryptographic Storage
Passwords hashed via BCRYPT adaptive hashing algorithm (`PASSWORD_BCRYPT` with auto-adjusting work factor), resisting GPU brute-force attacks.
A01:2021 Mitigation
4. Session Security & RBAC
Enforces HTTP-only session cookies, anti-fixation session ID regeneration upon login, and strict role validation (`requireAdmin()` / `requireLogin()`).
