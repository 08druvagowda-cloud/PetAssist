Emergency Veterinary Protocols

If your pet is in critical condition, stay calm and follow these immediate first-aid steps.

24/7 Animal Emergency Helpline: +91 98765 43210 / 1800-PET-EMERGENCY

Choking / Airway Block

Open mouth carefully to check for foreign object. If unconscious, apply gentle chest compressions.

Severe Bleeding

Apply direct pressure with a clean cloth. Elevate wound if possible. Do NOT use tourniquet without vet advice.
